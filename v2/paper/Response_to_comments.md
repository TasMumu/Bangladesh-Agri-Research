Manuscript: An Open Multi-Source Data Engineering Framework for District-Level Agricultural Vulnerability Assessment in Bangladesh

Thank you for the careful comments. While addressing them I re-audited the data pipeline and found three errors in the earlier version: BBS crop years were keyed one year early relative to the satellite and climate data, two BBS seasons had been dropped, and missing district-years had been filled by interpolation and NDVI-based estimates. The revised manuscript is therefore built only on BBS-observed yields (64 districts, harvest years 2011–2025, 960 district-years), and all results have been recomputed. Several conclusions changed; these are described in Section 4.1.

Other changes:

- All 14 BBS yearbook editions (2012–2025) were re-extracted. Each value is checked against unit identities and the printed national total, and compared across the two editions that print each year (Table 4).
- Flood exposure is now measured as two variables aligned to the Boro calendar: monsoon flooding before planting and April–May flash flooding before harvest. Each is computed from both JRC (Landsat) and Sentinel-1, and the two sources are compared (Figure 3).
- New finding: because BBS yield is measured per harvested hectare, flood damage appears in harvested area and production rather than yield (Tables 8–9).
- The reference list was rebuilt. Every entry was checked against Crossref or DataCite, and mismatched or unverifiable citations were removed.
- All numbers in the manuscript are generated by the code in the repository (folder v2); the pipeline reruns with one command.
